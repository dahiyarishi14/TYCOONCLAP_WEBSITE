Before You Ask

Common questions

How fast can this actually be live? +

Most projects go live within 7 days of kickoff, once we have your content and brand assets. Premium builds with custom copywriting can take slightly longer depending on scope.

What do you need from me to get started? +

Just your logo (if you have one), a few photos, and a short brief on what you offer. We'll write draft copy for you to review if you don't have existing content.

Do you handle hosting and the domain too? +

Yes — we can set up hosting and connect your existing domain, or help you register a new one. This is included in setup at no extra cost.

What happens after the site launches? +

Basic and Standard include a short post-launch check-in. Premium includes 30 days of support for edits, fixes, and small changes as you settle into the new site.

Why does a website cost this much? +

Freelance custom builds typically run $1,500–$8,000, and agencies charge $3,000–$15,000+. Our pricing sits below both because we've streamlined the process — you're still getting a fully custom design, not a $200 template.
