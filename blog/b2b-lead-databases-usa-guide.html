Cold Email Deliverability & CAN-SPAM Compliance

Under the United States CAN-SPAM Act, sending commercial B2B cold email is completely legal provided you observe essential hygiene rules:

1. Verify within 30 days: Every email in your database must be validated via SMTP handshakes and MX-record pings to maintain a bounce rate under 3%.

2. Provide accurate identification: Include your registered business address or virtual office location in your email footer.

3. Honor opt-outs immediately: Provide an automated unsubscribe link or simple text opt-out (*"Reply 'leave' to stop updates"*).

4. Use dedicated sending infrastructure: Never send cold volume from your primary company domain. Use secondary domains warmed up on tools like Instantly or Lemlist.
