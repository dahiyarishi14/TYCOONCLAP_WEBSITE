Privacy Policy

How Tycoon Clap collects, encrypts, and protects your information across global operations.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Tycoon Clap ("we," "us," "our") operates tycoonclap.com (the "Site") and provides Growth Marketing, Software Engineering, and Data & AI services, including our verified lead-list product on the Leads page. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the rights available to you under applicable law, including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA/CPRA").

By using this Site, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

1. Who we are

Data Controller: Tycoon Clap
Contact email: nathalie@tycoonclap.com

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Name, email address, and/or phone number submitted through our contact forms, requirement forms, or booking forms.
  • Message content you submit in any form field (e.g. project requirements, inquiries).
  • Payment-related information when purchasing a lead list on our Leads page — processed entirely by PayPal or Stripe; we do not receive or store your card or PayPal account credentials.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our third-party service providers automatically collect certain technical information via cookies and similar tracking technologies:

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and deliver services you request.
  • To process and deliver lead-list purchases.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site's performance and content.
  • To measure and optimize our advertising, including Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad campaigns.
  • To communicate with you about your inquiry, order, or our services.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Advertising cookies & Meta Business Tools

We may use Meta Pixel and/or the Meta Conversions API to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show you more relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram. This may involve sharing certain information about your interactions with our Site with Meta Platforms, Inc. Meta may combine this information with other data it holds about you in accordance with its own Data Policy.

You can control ad personalization directly through:

  • Meta Ad Preferences
  • Your browser's cookie settings
  • Industry opt-out tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page (US) or Your Online Choices (EU)

5. Legal basis for processing (GDPR — EU/UK visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for non-essential cookies (analytics, advertising) and marketing communications.
  • Contract — to respond to your inquiry or deliver a service/product you've purchased.
  • Legitimate interests — to maintain and secure the Site, and to understand aggregate usage patterns.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with law.

Your GDPR rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at nathalie@tycoonclap.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA) — this may include advertising cookies such as the Meta Pixel
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

We do not sell personal information for money. However, use of advertising technologies like Meta Pixel may be considered "sharing" under CPRA for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out at any time via the methods listed in Section 4, or by emailing us directly.

To submit a request, email nathalie@tycoonclap.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request."

7. Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site (Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Meta, PayPal, Stripe) under their own privacy terms
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where legally required
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets

8. International data transfers

Depending on where you access the Site from, your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries where we or our service providers operate (e.g. the United States, for Google, Meta, Microsoft, and PayPal infrastructure). Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses used by these providers.

9. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Form submissions and inquiry data are generally retained for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your request, unless you request earlier deletion.

10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for basic Site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity.
  • Advertising cookies — Meta Pixel, where active.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect Site functionality.

This policy is provided as a general template addressing GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Meta Business Tools disclosure requirements. It is not a substitute for legal advice. We recommend having this policy reviewed by a qualified attorney familiar with your specific operations, target markets, and the jurisdictions you serve.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
