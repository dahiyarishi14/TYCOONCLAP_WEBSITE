A story you'll recognize

He was bleeding $15,000 a month — and had no idea where

A growing US home-services business was spending on Google Ads, Facebook, and referral programs every month, but had no way to see which one actually paid off. Here's what that looked like from the inside — and what changed after 90 days of real analytics.

01 · Attribution

No idea which channel actually worked

Money went out to ads, social, and referrals every month — but nothing connected a closed sale back to the campaign that brought it in. We tied every channel into one dashboard, so each dollar is tracked straight through to revenue.

02 · Centralization

Data scattered across five different tools

Sales lived in the CRM, leads in a spreadsheet, ad performance in three more places — nobody had one number the whole team trusted. We pulled every source into a single real-time dashboard, updated automatically.

03 · Speed

Problems surfaced weeks too late

By the time a slump in leads showed up on the bank statement, it had already quietly cost a month of revenue. We set up automated alerts that flag a dip within days, not weeks.

04 · Profitability

No idea which customers were actually profitable

Some customers cost more to acquire than they ever paid back, but with no CAC or lifetime-value tracking, that was invisible. We built per-channel tracking so unprofitable spend gets cut fast.
